CCC / ADA Twin-Engine sharpening notes
========================================

1. Keep CCC and ADA separate until the prediction step.
   CCC is the conserved compression engine. ADA is the accounting engine. A claim that only invokes one side is incomplete.

2. Do not erase signs.
   Signed residuals and signed gradients are part of the information. Unsigned transformations may be useful diagnostics, but they are not the primary coordinate system.

3. Every prediction needs a failure condition.
   A paper should name the measurement that would count against it. If a claim cannot fail, it belongs in notes, not in the formal paper set.

4. Raw owner-supplied artifacts should remain traceable.
   Keep original filenames for CSV, TXT, and Python files. Add explanatory README entries rather than renaming source artifacts into more polished titles.

5. Keep research code out of product runtime.
   Decoder scripts and benchmark tools belong in this archive's code folder unless the owner explicitly asks to productize them.
