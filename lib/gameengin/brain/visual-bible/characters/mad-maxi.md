# Character: Mad Maxi

## Role
Player avatar. A small-stature scavenger-mechanic in a battered exo-suit two
sizes too large, daughter of a vanished engineer.

## Silhouette
- Top-heavy: oversized helm and pauldrons, slim legs.
- Asymmetric: left arm = grafted prosthetic with neon tube veins.
- Always carries a wrench-baton across the back.

## Palette
| Element | Hex |
|---------|-----|
| Suit base | `#3a3a44` |
| Suit accent | `#c8981a` |
| Visor glow | `#0ff` |
| Prosthetic neon | `#f0f` |

## Animation Identity
- Light, slightly off-balance walk (12 fps).
- Jump anticipation 4 frames; landing squash 6 frames.
- Idle: helmet tilts to inspect the prosthetic every 5 seconds.

## Voice
- Soft, dry, observational. Rarely shouts.
- Voiceover lines limited to <=12 words per beat.
