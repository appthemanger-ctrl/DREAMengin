# Principle: Progression

## Definition
The player must feel themselves growing — in capability, knowledge, or
narrative reach — on a measurable cadence.

## Cadence Targets
| Tier | Interval | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| Micro | every 30–90 s | Coin / XP tick, small unlock notification |
| Meso  | every 5–10 min | New move, new room, story beat |
| Macro | every 30–60 min | Boss defeat, biome change, ability gating lifted |

## Anti-Patterns
- Empty stretches of grinding with no novelty.
- Power spikes that obsolete prior abilities entirely.
- Hidden progression with no on-screen acknowledgement.
