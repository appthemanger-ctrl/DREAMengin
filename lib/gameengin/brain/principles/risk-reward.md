# Principle: Risk & Reward

## Definition
Every challenge offered to the player must carry a legible reward proportional
to the risk taken, and an optional safer path of comparable utility.

## Heuristics
| Risk Tier | Reward Multiplier | Visual Signal |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| Trivial   | 1.0× | Default world tint |
| Moderate  | 1.5× | Warm rim-light, mild particle wash |
| High      | 2.5× | Saturated accent + warning SFX motif |
| Extreme   | 4.0× | Full screen accent + sustained low-frequency rumble |

## Anti-Patterns
- Hidden lethal hazards with no telegraph.
- Maximum-risk rooms with cosmetic-only rewards.
- Mandatory high-risk segments with no safer fallback.
